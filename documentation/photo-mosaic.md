# Photos on the map

*Map › Tools › Photos › Photos on the map* (off until you turn it on) shows the
photos this app has received over MediaMesh, and the ones attached to team
pins and reports, as thumbnails where they were taken. Zoomed out, photos that
would overlap become one tile with a count. Tap a tile for its photos as a
timeline, newest first, with who sent each, when and where it came in; tap a
photo to see it full size. *All photos as a timeline* in the same sheet lists
every placed photo.

## Where a photo was taken

The photo itself carries no position, so the place comes from, best first:

1. **Its caption**: a position written in it. With *Tag my photos with where I
   am* on (off by default), the app adds `📍47.60378,-122.34052` to the caption
   of each photo you send, so everyone's map puts it in the right spot. Anyone
   who can see the photo can see that position.
2. **The pin or report it's attached to** (*Quick Pic* and photo reports).
3. **Roughly where its sender was at the time**: a teammate's recent track,
   your own track for photos you sent, or the sender's advertised position.
   These are marked "About here" and drawn slightly faded.

Photos with none of these aren't placed. Only photos already downloaded and
decodable on this phone show; a photo still to fetch appears once it's in.
